DuoBolt

High-performance duplicate file detection for macOS. Powered by a multi-threaded BLAKE3 engine, it scans folders, codebases, design archives, external drives, and NAS volumes with deterministic byte-for-byte accuracy. Review-first workflow ensures precision and reversibility.

Precise Detection

Byte-for-byte comparison without reliance on filenames, metadata, or heuristics. Results are consistent, verifiable, and scalable for professional use.

Review-First Workflow

Select locations, review duplicates in contextual groups, delete safely to Trash. All actions tracked with session-based Deletion History for easy restores.

APFS Clone Awareness

Detects macOS APFS clones—shared storage from cp, Time Machine, or caches—so reported space savings match actual disk frees. Toggleable and default-on.

Archive Instead of Delete

Move duplicates to any folder, preserving directory structure. Archives tracked in History with restore options; excluded from future scans by default.

Full Traceability

Deletion History logs every operation—freed space vs. archived—with review and cleanup tools. Deterministic across sessions and volumes.

How It Works

1. Select Locations

Choose folders, drives, or NAS volumes. Advanced options exclude archives and enable clone detection.

2. Scan and Group

BLAKE3 engine processes multi-threaded. Duplicates grouped by content with recoverable space shown accurately.

3. Review and Act

Inspect groups, select files. Delete to Trash, archive to folder, or restore from History.

4. Track and Restore

Every action logged. Review past sessions, restore files, clear recovered data.

FAQ

Is detection reliable across file types?

Yes. Pure byte-for-byte hashing with BLAKE3 ensures identical files are matched regardless of names, dates, or metadata.

What about large scans?

Optimized for any scale—multi-threaded, memory-efficient, handles codebases and NAS without slowdown.

Can I recover deleted files?

All deletions go to Trash. Full session history allows restores anytime.

Does it work on external drives?

Fully supported, including APFS clones where applicable. Non-APFS volumes behave identically.
